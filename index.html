Admission Process

📝 Step 1: Application

Fill online application form with personal and academic details. Application fee: ₹500

✍️ Step 2: Entrance Test

Appear for Excel Entrance Exam (EEE) - 2 hours, MCQ based. Syllabus: 10+2 level

🎤 Step 3: Interview

Personal interview with admission panel. Assess communication skills and career goals.

✅ Step 4: Enrollment

Document verification and fee payment. Join orientation program. Welcome to School!
